What is a native Salesforce ERP?

A native Salesforce ERP runs inside Salesforce on the same data model as your CRM, instead of syncing with a separate system. Ranger Fusion adds finance, inventory, procurement, projects and HR to Salesforce this way.

How long does Ranger Fusion take to implement?

Most teams go live in 2 to 4 weeks: configuration, data migration, training and cutover. There is no separate platform to set up and no CRM to ERP integration to build.

How much can we save compared with separate systems?

Teams typically save 20 to 60% against a patchwork of separate CRM, accounting, inventory and project tools, from fewer licences, no integration upkeep and less manual reconciliation.

Which modules does Ranger Fusion include?

Sales and quote-to-cash, finance and accounting, inventory and warehouse, procurement, projects and resources, HR, and AI agents that work across all of them.

Who is Ranger Fusion built for?

Growing and mid-market companies that already run sales or service on Salesforce and want finance and operations on the same platform, without buying and integrating a separate ERP.
