Our view

The licences pay back on data, not on activation.

Activating Now Assist skills is the quick part. Getting value from it depends on records most teams have not looked at closely in years: the knowledge base, the catalog, the work notes agents leave and the CMDB.

When pilots stall, the model is rarely the cause. The summaries are thin because the notes were thin, the answers are wrong because the article was wrong, and nobody can say what the AI costs or what it can see.

  • Stale knowledge

    Answers built on articles nobody has reviewed since they were written, with duplicates that disagree. Generative answers make the stale ones more convincing, not less.First move: retire, merge and re-own the articles behind the top request types

  • A catalog people cannot search

    Hundreds of items with vague names and overlapping forms. Virtual Agent and search can only route people to what exists and is described well.First move: retire unused items and rewrite the descriptions of the rest

  • Thin work notes

    Incidents closed with “fixed” and a generic resolution code. A summary of nothing is still nothing, and knowledge drafted from it is worse.First move: resolution standards for the desk before summaries go live

  • No oversight

    Every team wants AI first, so nothing ships, or several things ship with nobody tracking access, cost or accuracy.First move: AI Control Tower in place before the first agent, not after

Rules before go-live

Four rules every agent ships with.

  • People approve consequence.Actions that change records of consequence wait for a human, until accuracy on real tickets says otherwise.
  • Access reviewed first.What an agent can read and change is agreed with security before launch, not discovered after.
  • Cost tracked per agent.Spend is reported by agent and team from the first day of production.
  • A clear hand-off.Anything outside policy or confidence goes to a person, with the context already gathered.

Asked before switching on Now Assist.

What is ServiceNow Now Assist?

Now Assist is the generative AI built into the ServiceNow platform. It summarizes incidents and cases, powers Virtual Agent conversations, drafts knowledge articles and supports AI agents that act across workflows.

Why does the platform need a health check before Now Assist?

Now Assist reads the CMDB, tickets and knowledge base. If that data is duplicated or stale, the AI repeats the errors, so the platform is audited and cleaned first.

How long does it take to get a first Now Assist agent live?

A typical first engagement is a 4-week discovery, an 8-week build of the first production agent or copilot, and a 90-day platform setup so later use cases ship in weeks.

How is AI governed on ServiceNow?

AI Control Tower gives one view of every AI agent: token use and cost, performance, security posture and escalation controls. Procyon has implemented it in production.
