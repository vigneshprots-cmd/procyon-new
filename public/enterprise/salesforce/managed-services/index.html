Our view

The org is never finished, and Salesforce does not stand still.

Salesforce upgrades every org three times a year, ready or not. Meanwhile the business keeps asking for fields, reports and approval changes. Without a team that owns both, the backlog grows, changes get made straight in production, and within a couple of years the org needs the audit it was built to avoid.

Managed services is the discipline that prevents that drift: every change through a sandbox, every release previewed before it lands, and a fixed share of each month spent on improvement rather than tickets.

  • Changes made in production

    A quick fix for an urgent request, made directly in the live org, with no record of why. Multiply by a few hundred and nobody can explain the org.First move: a sandbox for every change and a documented deployment path

  • Releases nobody reads

    Each release changes behaviour somewhere, and some features switch on by default. Orgs that never test the preview find out from their users.First move: regression tests in a preview sandbox about a month ahead

  • A backlog that only grows

    Tickets crowd out improvement, so the requests that would save the most time never reach the top.First move: a monthly backlog prioritized with the business owner

  • One admin, no cover

    A single in-house admin holds the org in their head. Holidays stall the queue and a resignation becomes a rescue.First move: a named team, with every change and integration documented

Asked before signing a managed services retainer.

What are Salesforce managed services?

An ongoing retainer in which a partner runs your Salesforce org: user support, administration, release management, monitoring and continuous improvement. Procyon offers three tiers, Run, Improve and Scale, each including the one before.

How much do Salesforce managed services cost?

Cost depends on the number of users, the clouds you run and how much change you need each month. Procyon writes each quote for your org after a short call, with no long lock-in.

How do you handle Salesforce's three releases a year?

We test the Spring, Summer and Winter release previews in your sandbox about a month before each upgrade, fix anything that would break, and plan which new features to adopt.

What hours does your support team cover?

Our hubs in Novato, Toronto and Chennai cover North American and Indian working hours, so requests logged late in a US day can be worked on overnight.

Can we switch to you from our current Salesforce partner?

Yes. We shadow your current partner, document the org, run a quick health check and take over on an agreed date, so support continues without a gap.

Is managed services better than hiring an admin?

A retainer gives you admins, developers and an architect for less than building that team in-house, with cover for holidays and turnover. Many clients keep one internal admin and use us for the rest.
