Asked before applying.

Where are Procyon's teams based?

Procyon's teams work from three hubs: headquarters in Novato, California, North America delivery in Toronto, Canada, and the global delivery center in Chennai, India.

Which roles are open at Procyon?

Open now: AI Architect (Java Background with AI Fundamentals), Full Stack Developer (Backend-Focused), QA Engineer (Manual + API Testing) and Content and Copy Writer, all full-time and based in Chennai. Open applications are welcome for every track.

Which roles does Procyon hire for?

Salesforce admins, developers, consultants and architects; ServiceNow developers and consultants; AI, integration and data engineers; web, mobile and cloud engineers; and program managers, business analysts and QA.

Can I apply if no open role is listed?

Yes. Choose the closest track, or Other, and send an application. Applications are matched to programs as they start.

Is a CV required?

No. A LinkedIn profile or portfolio link is enough to start. The talent team asks for more detail when there is a match.

Can I apply with a personal email address?

Yes. Personal email addresses are accepted on the careers application. Temporary or disposable addresses are not.
