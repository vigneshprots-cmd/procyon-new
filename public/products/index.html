How the products are built

Product discipline, delivery experience.

  • Born in delivery.Each product started as a pattern Procyon's teams rebuilt for client after client, then hardened into software.
  • Live in weeks.Products are configured to the operation, not rebuilt for it, so go-live is measured in weeks.
  • One team behind it.The people who build the product deploy it and support it, so there is no hand-off between vendor and integrator.
  • Configured per site.Rules, languages, branding and integrations are settings, so the same product fits different operations.

Questions about Procyon products.

What products does Procyon Technostructure build?

Procyon builds two products. Ranger Fusion is an AI-powered ERP built natively on Salesforce that runs sales, accounting, inventory, procurement, projects, HR and portals on one data model. Procyon WFM is workforce management software that schedules shifts against rest, hours and certification rules and runs attendance, replacements, overtime and payroll export.

How quickly can a Procyon product go live?

Ranger Fusion typically goes live in 2 to 4 weeks from kickoff. Procyon WFM is configured to each site's lines, shifts and rules, and a demo runs a live week against the operation's own data before any commitment.

Who supports the products after go-live?

The same Procyon team that builds and deploys each product supports it, from Novato in California, Toronto and Chennai, so there is one accountable party for the software and the rollout.
