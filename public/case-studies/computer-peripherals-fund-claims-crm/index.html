Case study · Hi-Tech Manufacturing

Fund and claims management on Salesforce for a $4.5B peripherals manufacturer

A leading computer peripherals manufacturer with ~$4.5B in annual revenue had no fund-tracking system for promotions and campaigns. Procyon built a custom Salesforce-based platform to create projects and activities, allocate funds through multi-step approvals, and automate claim processing.

The situation

What was holding the business back.

  • Business had no fund-tracking system for promotions and campaigning.
  • Sales and marketing users needed to create projects and activities and assign funds routed through multiple approvals.
  • Claim processing was heavily manual, with intervention required across the workflow.

How the program was run.

WorkstreamCustom project & fund appBuilt a custom Salesforce interface to create projects and activities and assign funds, with dynamic approvals and fund-availability checking.
WorkstreamClaims automationAutomated claim processing end-to-end and synchronized data with Oracle systems for accrual management.
WorkstreamFinance + commercial alignmentGave finance users the ability to create funds and trace utilization, and enabled sales and marketing to allocate funds to specific activities.
WorkstreamOpen & integrated systemDelivered an open system to manage funds for promotion and campaigning activities, claims and accruals across the enterprise.
The result

What changed after go-live.

  • Fund data visibility across the enterprise.
  • Finance users can create funds and trace fund utilization.
  • Sales and marketing users can create projects and activities with allocated funds.
  • Open system to manage funds on promotion and campaigning activities, with claims processing integrated to Oracle for accrual processing.
