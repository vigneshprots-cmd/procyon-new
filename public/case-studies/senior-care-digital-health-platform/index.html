Reinventing in-home senior care for a US digital health platform 34

A US-based digital health platform revolutionizing elder care by connecting families with trusted, background-checked in-home caregivers, Procyon delivered the Salesforce + MuleSoft + web & mobile stack behind it.

The situation

What was holding the business back.

  • Families struggled to find trusted, background-checked in-home caregivers for elderly relatives.
  • Traditional senior care models were expensive and opaque, with limited flexibility for families and caregivers.
  • Caregiver onboarding, scheduling and family communication were fragmented across multiple disconnected tools.

How the program was run.

WorkstreamSalesforce coreBuilt the platform of record for caregivers, families and care plans on Salesforce, with workflows for vetting, onboarding and matching.
WorkstreamMuleSoft integrationIntegrated background-check, payments and scheduling systems through a MuleSoft API layer so every channel speaks the same language.
WorkstreamWeb & mobile experiencesDelivered consumer-grade web and mobile apps for families and caregivers, with transparent pricing and flexible-hours scheduling.
WorkstreamOperational visibilityStood up dashboards giving the operations team a single view of demand, caregiver utilization and family satisfaction across all states.
The result

What changed after go-live.

  • Enabled 50% savings over traditional care models.
  • Increased caregiver satisfaction through transparent pay and flexible hours.
  • Improved family trust and engagement with a secure, all-in-one management platform.
  • Helped 1,500+ families access quality senior care with minimal friction.
