07 Questions

What buyers ask us first.

Something else? sales@procyonts.com

What does Procyon Technostructure do?

Procyon is a Salesforce and ServiceNow consulting partner. We implement, integrate, fix and manage both platforms, and add AI (Agentforce, Now Assist), data and integration services around them. We also build Ranger Fusion, an ERP that runs natively on Salesforce.

Is Procyon a certified Salesforce and ServiceNow partner?

Yes. We are a Salesforce Consulting Partner and a ServiceNow Implementation Partner, certified to ISO 27001 and ISO 9001, with HIPAA-compliant delivery for healthcare clients.

How long does an Agentforce implementation take?

With our Agent Launch Kit, your first agents for lead qualification, case triage or self-service can be in production in under four weeks. Larger multi-agent programs are phased by use case.

Can you fix an existing Salesforce or ServiceNow implementation?

Yes, it is a large share of our work. We start with a health check covering data quality, automation sprawl, integrations and license use, then remove the technical debt in a fixed-scope engagement, typically eight weeks for ServiceNow.

Do you provide Salesforce and ServiceNow staff augmentation?

Yes. We place certified administrators, developers, consultants and architects, individually or as delivery pods, working in US, Canadian or Indian time zones.

What engagement models do you offer?

Fixed-scope projects, time and materials, outcome-based contracts tied to business KPIs, and managed services for ongoing support and optimization.

Where is Procyon located?

We are headquartered in Novato, California, with delivery centers in Toronto, Canada and Chennai, India. Procyon was founded in 2004 and has more than 200 people.
