Straight answers.

What does Procyon actually do?

We audit, implement, integrate and run Salesforce and ServiceNow, connect them to your data and ERP, and deploy AI agents such as Agentforce and Now Assist inside them. We also supply certified talent and build Ranger Fusion, an ERP native to Salesforce.

What do I get from a platform audit?

A written plan: what is broken, what it costs you, what to fix first, plus effort, timeline and a cost range. It starts with a free 30-minute call with a senior architect.

How fast can AI agents go live?

With our Agent Launch Kit, first agents for qualification, case triage or self-service can be in production in under four weeks, if the data underneath is sound. The audit tells you whether it is.

Can you take over a failing implementation?

Yes, rescue is a large share of our work. A typical ServiceNow tech-debt removal runs eight weeks. On Salesforce we stabilize the org first, then rebuild what is blocking adoption.

Do you provide Salesforce and ServiceNow people?

Yes. Certified admins, developers, consultants and architects, individually or as delivery pods, in US, Canadian or Indian time zones.

Are you a certified partner?

We are a Salesforce Consulting Partner and a ServiceNow Implementation Partner, certified to ISO 27001 and ISO 9001, with HIPAA-compliant delivery.
