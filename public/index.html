Straight answers.

What does Procyon Technostructure do?

Procyon is an AI technology company. We engineer AI agents such as Agentforce and Now Assist into Salesforce and ServiceNow, build the data and integrations they depend on, and audit, implement and run the platforms themselves. We also supply certified talent and build Procyon Fusion, an ERP native to Salesforce.

What services do you offer beyond Salesforce and ServiceNow?

Digital transformation, product engineering, enterprise web and mobility, application maintenance and support, cloud, and big data and analytics. One team can own the platform, the custom build around it and the support afterwards.

What happens after I reach out?

A senior architect replies within one business day, runs a 30-minute call on your platform and goals, then sends a written plan with scope, timeline and a cost range. Yours to keep.

How fast can AI agents go live?

With our Agent Launch Kit, first agents for qualification, case triage or self-service can be in production in under four weeks, if the data underneath is sound. The first call tells you whether it is.

Can you take over a failing implementation?

Yes, rescue is a large share of our work. A typical ServiceNow tech-debt removal runs eight weeks. On Salesforce we stabilize the org first, then rebuild what is blocking adoption.

Do you provide Salesforce and ServiceNow people?

Yes. Certified admins, developers, consultants and architects, individually or as delivery pods, in US, Canadian or Indian time zones.

Which industries do you work in?

Mostly regulated, high-volume ones: healthcare and life sciences, financial services, public sector, retail and high tech. We have also incubated two AI healthcare start-ups of our own, Hekma and Akra.

Are you a certified partner?

We are a Salesforce Consulting Partner and a ServiceNow Implementation Partner, certified to ISO 27001 and ISO 9001:2015, with HIPAA-compliant delivery. Procyon is also WBE, NMSDC and SBE certified.
