At Dreamforce 2026 you will not find Procyon behind a booth handing out swag. You will find our senior Salesforce architects in private, working-session-style meetings with enterprise leaders deciding their next move on Agentforce, Data Cloud, or the next phase of their Salesforce roadmap. We come to Dreamforce to listen, whiteboard, and leave you with a plan you can act on the following week - no demos, no pitch, no fluff. Here is what a session looks like and how to book one.

IN SHORT

Procyon offers a limited number of private 30-minute Salesforce strategy sessions across all three days of Dreamforce 2026. Each is led by a senior architect, focused entirely on your situation - Agentforce, Data Cloud, Service Cloud, or MuleSoft integration - with no product pitch. You leave with a tailored architecture view and a free post-event 30-day acceleration plan. Slots are deliberately limited to keep every session senior, and they fill quickly, so booking early is the way to secure a time that fits around your agenda.

Why a working session beats a booth

Booths are built to broadcast; a working session is built to solve. Instead of watching a scripted demo on someone else's data, you get a senior architect looking at your situation - your estate, your data, your roadmap - and reacting in real time. In thirty minutes you can pressure-test an Agentforce plan, sanity-check a Data Cloud approach, or get a candid second opinion on an implementation already in flight. That is value you cannot get from a keynote or a hallway conversation, and it is why we structure our Dreamforce presence around meetings rather than a stand.
