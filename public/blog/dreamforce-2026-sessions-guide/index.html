With more than 1,500 sessions across three days, the real challenge at Dreamforce 2026 is not finding things to do - it is avoiding the trap of doing a lot and learning little. A packed calendar of keynotes feels productive and rarely is. Here is how to build an agenda around decisions rather than FOMO, so you leave with a plan instead of a tote bag.

Decide what you came to decide

Before you open the session catalog, write down the one or two questions you must answer by Thursday - 'Should we build Agentforce on Service Cloud this year?' or 'Is Data Cloud the right foundation for our AI roadmap?' Every session choice then becomes easy: does it move that decision, or not?

A sane three-day structure

Day 1 - orient and go deep. Catch the opening keynote for announcements, then spend the afternoon in one or two hands-on workshops on your priority topic, likely Agentforce or Data Cloud.

Day 2 - learn from peers and decide. Prioritize customer-story sessions and roadmap deep-dives. Use the day to stress-test your assumptions against people who have shipped.

Day 3 - convert to a plan. Lighter on sessions, heavier on working meetings. Turn the week's insights into concrete next steps before you leave the city.

THE KEYNOTE RULE
Keynotes are recorded and easy to catch up on. Do not burn a prime-time slot in one when you could be in a working session or a 1:1 you cannot replay. Watch the keynote on the flight home.

The sessions the catalog will not give you

The most valuable conversations at Dreamforce are rarely on the schedule. They are the private, working-session-style meetings where someone who has taken agents to production looks at your actual data and roadmap and tells you what will and will not work. That is exactly what Procyon runs across all three days - see the Dreamforce 2026 agenda and book a slot.

Questions this article answers.

How many sessions are at Dreamforce 2026?

Dreamforce typically features more than 1,500 sessions across three days, far more than anyone can attend. Value comes from curating around one or two decisions rather than trying to see everything.

Which Dreamforce sessions are most valuable for IT leaders?

Hands-on workshops and customer-story sessions tend to deliver the most, because they offer real product depth and honest accounts of what worked. Keynotes are recorded, so watch them later.

How should I plan my three days at Dreamforce?

Orient and go deep on day one, learn from peers and decide on day two, and convert insights into a plan on day three, with private working meetings booked in advance.
