Corporate supplier diversity programs are under more pressure than they have ever been. Procurement leaders are asked to grow diverse spend, prove it with clean data, and do it without slowing a single enterprise program. That is a difficult balance, and it is exactly where the right technology partner earns its place.

The short answer: working with a certified minority business enterprise (MBE) that delivers enterprise Salesforce programs lets you direct real, high value technology spend toward your Tier 1 and Tier 2 diversity goals while keeping delivery quality high. Procyon Technostructure is a certified MBE, and we built our Salesforce practice for exactly this kind of mandate.

Why supplier diversity and Salesforce belong in the same conversation

Salesforce programs are large, recurring, and strategic. In most enterprises they sit with a small number of big integrators, which means the spend rarely touches a diverse supplier. Redirecting even part of that budget to a certified MBE partner moves your numbers in a way that smaller, one off purchases never will. You are not looking for token spend. You are looking for qualified, defensible spend on work that matters, and Salesforce delivery fits that description perfectly.

What Tier 1 and Tier 2 actually require

Tier 1 spend is money you pay directly to a certified diverse supplier. Tier 2 spend is diverse spend that flows through your prime contractors. A certified MBE Salesforce partner can serve both paths. We can engage directly as your Tier 1 implementation partner, or we can sit under an existing prime or systems integrator as a Tier 2 subcontractor, so your current program keeps moving while your diversity numbers improve.

The real worry: will delivery quality suffer?

This is the honest question every procurement leader asks, and it deserves an honest answer. A certified MBE classification is a statement about ownership, not a ceiling on capability. Procyon brings more than 20 years of enterprise delivery, over 200 completed projects for 75 plus global clients, three global delivery centers, and ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certified delivery. You can see how we are structured on our company page. The diversity credential and the delivery credential are both real, and you do not have to trade one for the other.

How to make the switch without disruption

Changing or adding a Salesforce partner feels risky, so we keep it low risk and evidence based.

  • Start with a scoped engagement that has a clear, measurable outcome, not a full re-platforming.
  • Run it in parallel with your current work so nothing in flight is disturbed.
  • Agree the success metric up front, the same discipline we describe in our guide to the first 90 days with a Salesforce partner.
  • Expand only once the first result is proven.

If you are formally evaluating partners, our list of questions to ask a Salesforce implementation partner works just as well for a diverse supplier as for an incumbent.

Where NMSDC 2026 fits

The NMSDC Annual Conference and Exchange in Los Angeles is where corporate buyers and certified MBEs actually meet and build relationships. It is the most efficient place to pressure test whether a diverse Salesforce partner can carry real enterprise work. Procyon will be there, and you can plan a conversation in advance on our NMSDC 2026 page so the time in Los Angeles is spent on your priorities rather than introductions.

Frequently asked questions

What is a certified MBE Salesforce partner?
It is a Salesforce implementation and managed services firm that is certified as a minority business enterprise, so the work it delivers can count toward your supplier diversity goals while meeting enterprise delivery standards.

Does using an MBE partner affect delivery quality?
No. The MBE certification reflects ownership, not capability. A qualified MBE partner brings the same certifications, methods, and senior talent as any strong integrator.

How does MBE Salesforce spend count toward Tier 2 goals?
If the MBE partner delivers as a subcontractor under one of your prime vendors, that spend is reported as Tier 2 diverse spend, which lets you improve your numbers without replacing your prime.
