Dreamforce is the largest AI and CRM event in the world, and Dreamforce 2026 arrives at a pivotal moment: the year the agentic enterprise moves from keynote slogan to production reality. For an enterprise IT leader, three days in San Francisco can be a blur of keynotes and swag, or the fastest way to pressure-test your Salesforce and AI roadmap with people who have shipped it. This guide covers the essentials and, more importantly, how to make the trip pay off.

What Dreamforce is, and what it is not

Dreamforce is part product launch, part industry gathering, part strategy summit. Expect keynotes on Agentforce and Data Cloud, hundreds of breakouts, hands-on workshops, and partner activity across the city. What it is not is a place to passively absorb. The value comes from the conversations and decisions you drive, not the sessions you sit through.

The 2026 theme: the agentic enterprise

Every Dreamforce has a center of gravity, and 2026's is unmistakable: autonomous AI agents doing real work alongside people. Expect the narrative to be that agents are inevitable and easy. The reality on the ground, where an experienced implementer earns their keep, is that agents succeed or fail on data readiness, scope, and governance, not on the model. Gartner still expects over 40% of agentic AI projects to be cancelled by 2027 - a useful reality check to carry into the keynote.

How to turn three days into real value

  1. Go in with a decision, not a wishlist. Name the one or two questions you must answer and aim every session at them.

  2. Prioritize working sessions over keynotes. Keynotes are recorded; a whiteboard with someone who has solved your problem is not.

  3. Book your key meetings before you arrive. The best people are fully booked by the opening bell.

  4. Plan the follow-through now. Decide who owns turning insights into a plan the week after.
