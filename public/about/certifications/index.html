Is Procyon a Salesforce partner?

Yes. Procyon Technostructure is a Salesforce Consulting Partner, with Administrator, Platform Developer, Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Revenue Cloud, Data Cloud and Einstein AI certifications across the team.

Is Procyon a ServiceNow partner?

Yes. Procyon is a ServiceNow Implementation Partner, delivering ITSM, ITOM, CMDB, Security Operations and GRC programs and tech-debt removal.

Is Procyon ISO 27001 certified?

Yes. Procyon is certified to ISO 27001 for information security management and ISO 9001:2015 for quality management, and holds a SOC 2 Type II attestation.

Does Procyon deliver HIPAA-compliant work?

Yes. Delivery for healthcare and life sciences clients follows HIPAA-compliant practices, with Salesforce Shield encryption where a program requires it.

Which supplier diversity certifications does Procyon hold?

Procyon is a WBENC-certified women's business enterprise and is certified through NMSDC and as an SBE, so qualifying spend can count toward supplier diversity programs.
