Asked about Procyon before the first call.

What is Procyon Technostructure?

Procyon Technostructure is an enterprise solution delivery partner founded in 2004. It audits, implements, integrates and runs Salesforce and ServiceNow, connects them to data and ERP systems, deploys AI agents inside them, supplies certified talent and builds Ranger Fusion, an ERP native to Salesforce.

Where is Procyon based?

Procyon is headquartered at 7250 Redwood Blvd, Suite #211, Novato, California, with delivery hubs in Scarborough, Toronto, Canada and on Anna Salai in Chennai, India.

How large is Procyon?

More than 200 professionals, serving more than 200 enterprise clients, from Fortune 500 companies to high-growth start-ups, across the United States, Canada and India.

Which platforms does Procyon work on?

Salesforce, including Agentforce, Data Cloud and MuleSoft, and ServiceNow, plus AI agents, data integration with SAP, Oracle, NetSuite and Workday, and custom product engineering.

Is Procyon a certified partner?

Yes. Procyon is a Salesforce Consulting Partner and a ServiceNow Implementation Partner, certified to ISO 27001 and ISO 9001:2015, with SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA-compliant delivery. It is also WBENC, NMSDC and SBE certified.

Which industries does Procyon serve?

Mostly regulated, high-volume industries: healthcare and life sciences, financial services, public sector, retail and high tech. Procyon has also incubated two AI healthcare ventures: Hekma, which accelerates drug development with a focus on patient equity, and Akra, which scales regulated digital healthcare products.
