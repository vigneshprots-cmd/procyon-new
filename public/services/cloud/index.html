Asked before a cloud move.

AWS, Azure or Google Cloud: which is best?

Whichever best fits the workloads, skills and ISV ecosystem. Procyon is certified across all three and designs multi-cloud-ready landing zones. The first decision is workload by workload, not vendor first.

How are runaway cloud bills avoided?

FinOps is built in from day one: tagging, budget alerts, anomaly detection, rightsizing, savings plans and unit economics per business capability. Most clients see a 30 to 40% cost reduction within two quarters.

Can complex migrations happen with minimal downtime?

Yes. A 6Rs assessment classifies every workload, then wave-based execution, automated cutover and blue-green strategies keep downtime close to zero. More than 300 workloads have been moved, including SAP, Oracle DB and large data estates.

How is security handled across multiple clouds?

Through a unified landing zone with identity-first access, CSPM and CWPP across clouds, secrets management and centralized logging into the SIEM, so compliance is continuous.

What happens after the migration?

24x7 SRE-led managed cloud services cover incident response, FinOps, security posture, capacity planning and continuous modernization. Most clients move to managed run within 90 days of go-live.
