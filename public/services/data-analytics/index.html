Asked before modernizing a data platform.

Which data platform should we standardize on?

Procyon works across Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery and Synapse. The right choice depends on workloads, existing investments and ecosystem, so the options are scored on total cost of ownership, performance, governance and team fit before a recommendation is made.

Is a semantic layer still needed with modern BI tools?

Yes, for any organization that wants one definition per metric. A governed semantic layer keeps KPIs consistent across Tableau, Power BI, Looker and AI tools, so teams stop arguing about whose number is right.

How is data governance and privacy handled?

Through a catalog, lineage, quality rules and access controls designed into the platform, aligned with GDPR, CCPA and HIPAA, so sensitive data is governed before it reaches dashboards or AI.

Can the data platform feed AI and automation?

Yes. Trusted data products are built to serve dashboards, AI agents, knowledge assistants and Salesforce Data Cloud from the same governed source.

What results do data platform programs deliver?

Across more than 100 data platforms delivered, reporting has become up to 80% faster and self-service adoption has grown up to tenfold. For Glendale Water & Power, 10+ utility systems were unified with a 15-day megawatt forecasting pipeline.
