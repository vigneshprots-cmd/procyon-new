Asked before a security engagement.

What is SOC in a Box?

A 24x7 managed detection and response service across cloud, on-premises and hybrid environments: unified telemetry, AI-assisted triage and analysts who confirm severity, block IPs and isolate endpoints. It onboards in 2 to 3 weeks with asset-based pricing.

How much does SOC in a Box cost compared with building a SOC?

Roughly one-fifth of the cost of a comparable enterprise SOC stack, with the platform and 24x7 service included and pricing based on the assets covered.

How does continuous compliance work?

Controls are mapped continuously to HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001, NIST CSF and PCI DSS, evidence is collected automatically, and failed controls are flagged, assigned and tracked to remediation.

Is Procyon a reseller of security tools?

No. Third-party tool costs are passed through at zero markup. Fees cover expertise, not margin on software.

What does the free first session involve?

A complimentary two-hour exploration of the environment, the concerns and the tools in place, ending with the likely gaps, quick wins and whether an assessment, SOC or GRC service fits. There is no pitch and no obligation.

Does Procyon secure public-sector environments?

Yes. Zero Trust, continuous monitoring and RMF-aligned work is delivered for public-sector clients, with FedRAMP-aligned delivery on AWS GovCloud and Azure Government.
