Asked before adding people to a team.

How fast can Procyon place a Salesforce or ServiceNow consultant?

A shortlist of vetted candidates arrives within 72 hours for most roles, and the average time from request to an onboarded consultant is 7 to 14 days. Niche, cleared or highly specialized roles can take longer, with the timeline agreed upfront.

How are candidates vetted?

Every candidate passes a technical screen, a scenario-based interview, a communication assessment and reference checks. Placements are backed by a no-questions-asked replacement guarantee.

Which engagement models are available for staff augmentation?

Time and materials, fixed-price pods, milestone-based and managed capacity, with onshore, nearshore (Canada) and offshore (India) blends at predictable rates.

Can the team scale up or down quickly?

Yes. The bench and active sourcing networks allow teams to scale up or down within two weeks. Most clients keep a steady core team plus a flex layer that follows demand.

Is government-cleared talent available?

Yes. Procyon maintains a pool of US-cleared resources for federal, state and local engagements, including FedRAMP-aligned delivery and public-trust roles.

Which skills can Procyon supply?

Salesforce, ServiceNow, SAP, Workday and Oracle specialists; AWS, Azure, GCP, Snowflake, Databricks and AI engineers; full-stack, mobile, DevOps and QA automation engineers; and architects, project managers, business analysts and scrum masters.
