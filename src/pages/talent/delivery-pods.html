What one Procyon team changed.

Asked before starting a delivery pod.

What is a dedicated delivery pod?

A delivery pod is a cross-functional team, typically an architect, developers, a business analyst and QA, that works as one squad on a client roadmap with shared accountability for sprint outcomes, rather than individual people billed by the hour.

How is a pod different from staff augmentation?

With staff augmentation, people join the client team and the client directs the work. A delivery pod owns the sprint outcomes as a team, arrives with all the roles it needs and can be contracted on fixed-price or milestone terms.

How quickly can a pod scale?

Capacity can be added or released within two weeks. Most clients keep a steady core team plus a flex layer that follows demand.

Which commercial terms are available for pods?

Time and materials, fixed-price pods, milestone-based and managed capacity, with onshore, nearshore (Canada) and offshore (India) blends at predictable rates.

Which platforms can a pod work on?

Salesforce, ServiceNow, AI and data including MuleSoft and Data Cloud, and custom product engineering. Pods can also mix platforms when a roadmap crosses more than one.
