The work

Client platforms that run in production.

Every track works on client platforms in production, next to senior practitioners. The people who scope a program are the people who build it, so the architect who wrote the plan is the one reviewing the work.

That has a cost. Salesforce ships three releases a year and ServiceNow two, and the clients depend on every change going in cleanly. The upside is that what is learned here is learned on real systems.

  • Orgs other people built

    A large share of Salesforce work is rescue: reading an org another partner built, finding the automation that fights itself, and fixing it without stopping the business.Where it leads: knowing a platform well enough to fix it, not only configure it

  • Handover across time zones

    Work moves between Novato, Toronto and Chennai, so a change raised at the end of a California day is often tested by the next morning.Where it leads: writing things down so someone else can pick them up

  • Senior people who stay

    The architect who scopes a program stays through go-live and hypercare, so questions are answered by the person who made the design decision.Where it leads: learning design from the people doing it

  • Certifications that get used

    Administrator, Platform Developer, Sales and Service Cloud Consultant, Revenue Cloud, Data Cloud and Einstein AI certifications are held across the practice and used on live programs.Where it leads: certifications earned next to the work, not instead of it

Can I apply if no open role is listed?

Yes. Choose the closest track, or Other, and send an application. Applications are matched to programs as they start.

No. A LinkedIn profile or portfolio link is enough to start. The talent team asks for more detail when there is a match.

Can I apply with a personal email address?

Yes. Personal email addresses are accepted on the careers application. Temporary or disposable addresses are not.
