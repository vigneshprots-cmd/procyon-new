Asked by CIOs and platform owners.

How long does a ServiceNow platform health check take?

A typical platform health check runs 2 to 3 weeks. CMDB integrity, workflow configurations, integration health and licensed utilization are audited, then a prioritized remediation plan is delivered. There is no commitment to further work.

Can Procyon rescue a stalled ServiceNow implementation?

Yes. Most platforms Procyon is brought into run at 30 to 40% of licensed capacity, with broken CMDB data and orphaned workflows. The gaps are documented, then remediated on a fixed SOW before any new module goes live.

Does Procyon work with federal and state agencies?

Yes. Procyon has delivered for the Administrative Office of the Courts, NIH, the VA, the U.S. Department of Transportation and the City of Glendale Water and Power, and holds ISO 27001, ISO 9001, HIPAA, WBE, SBE and CMAS credentials.

How is ServiceNow work priced?

On a zero-markup model: a fixed SOW with transparent deliverables, and technology costs passed through at cost. There is no managed services lock-in.

Which ServiceNow modules does Procyon implement?

ITSM, ITOM and CMDB, Security Operations, GRC, Customer Service and Field Service Management, HR Service Delivery, Now Assist and AI Control Tower, plus App Engine and IntegrationHub.
