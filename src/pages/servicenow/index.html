Our view

The licence is rarely the problem. The customization is.

ServiceNow ships two family releases a year. An instance built close to the platform takes them in weeks. One built around a previous partner's scripts takes them in quarters, and the team stops upgrading until support pressure forces it.

That is why we audit before we build. The same customizations that slow upgrades also corrupt the CMDB, confuse the service desk and leave Now Assist reading data nobody would sign off on.

  • Upgrades that take a quarter

    Base-system business rules, script includes and forms edited in place. Each upgrade skips them, and the skipped list grows release after release until nobody reviews it.First move: a skipped-record review with a revert, keep or rebuild call on each

  • A CMDB nobody trusts

    CIs loaded by spreadsheet and integration, bypassing identification rules, with no owner per class. Change impact becomes a guess and incident routing follows the wrong support group.First move: a CMDB Health baseline and a CSDM target model

  • Processes copied from the old tool

    Incident categories nobody uses, a change advisory board that reviews every change, problem records opened and never closed. The platform works, the process design does not.First move: change models with risk-based approval and a standard change catalog

  • AI ahead of the data

    Now Assist summarizes work notes, drafts knowledge and answers through Virtual Agent. If knowledge is stale and the catalog is cluttered, it repeats both, faster.First move: a knowledge and catalog review before skills are switched on

Asked by CIOs and platform owners.

How long does a ServiceNow platform health check take?

A typical platform health check runs 2 to 3 weeks. CMDB integrity, workflow configurations, integration health and licensed utilization are audited, then a prioritized remediation plan is delivered. There is no commitment to further work.

Can Procyon rescue a stalled ServiceNow implementation?

Yes. Most platforms Procyon is brought into run at 30 to 40% of licensed capacity, with broken CMDB data and orphaned workflows. The gaps are documented, then remediated on a fixed SOW before any new module goes live.

Does Procyon work with federal and state agencies?

Yes. Procyon has delivered for the Administrative Office of the Courts, NIH, the VA, the U.S. Department of Transportation and the City of Glendale Water and Power, and holds ISO 27001, ISO 9001, HIPAA, WBE, SBE and CMAS credentials.

How is ServiceNow work priced?

On a zero-markup model: a fixed SOW with transparent deliverables, and technology costs passed through at cost. There is no managed services lock-in.

Which ServiceNow modules does Procyon implement?

ITSM, ITOM and CMDB, Security Operations, GRC, Customer Service and Field Service Management, HR Service Delivery, Now Assist and AI Control Tower, plus App Engine and IntegrationHub.
