What do ServiceNow managed services include?

Administration and user requests, upgrade readiness for both yearly family releases, CMDB hygiene, integration monitoring, a monthly enhancement backlog and Now Assist tuning.

Is there a lock-in?

No. Work is scoped on a transparent SOW with technology costs passed through at cost, and there is no managed services lock-in.

Which hours does the run team cover?

Hubs in Novato, Toronto and Chennai provide 24/7 global delivery support across North American and Indian time zones.

Can Procyon take over from another ServiceNow partner?

Yes. The current setup is shadowed and documented, a quick health check flags inherited risks, and support moves across on an agreed date.
