Asked by agencies and prime integrators.

Is Procyon FedRAMP-aligned?

Yes. Procyon delivers to FedRAMP Moderate and High baselines on AWS GovCloud and Azure Government, and aligns program governance to FISMA, RMF and agency-specific requirements.

Which contract vehicles does Procyon support?

GSA Schedule and major IDIQs, plus subcontracting under prime integrators. Deals are structured quickly under the right vehicle for the agency, and CMAS is available for California public-sector buyers.

Can Procyon provide cleared resources?

Yes, including Public Trust, Secret and Top Secret cleared resources across Salesforce, ServiceNow, SAP, cloud, data and AI practices.

How is citizen-facing modernization handled?

Through Salesforce Public Sector Solutions and ServiceNow Citizen Experience platforms, with omnichannel service, case management, identity verification and Section 508 and WCAG accessibility built in.

How are long public-sector programs de-risked?

Agile delivery in 90-day increments, value milestones tied to mission outcomes, transparent burndowns, ATO-aware engineering and continuous alignment with sponsors and end users.

Does working with Procyon help primes meet subcontracting goals?

Procyon is a WBENC-certified women's business enterprise and is NMSDC and SBE certified, so qualifying subcontracted work can count toward small business and supplier diversity goals.
