Our view

Programs stall on authorization, rarely on technology.

The platform is seldom the problem. Programs slip because the authorization package is written after the build, because accessibility is tested at the end, and because sponsors cannot see progress until a milestone is missed.

Each of those is a sequencing decision, and each is cheaper to make in the first month than to recover from in the eighteenth. Compliance designed in from discovery is the difference between an ATO that arrives with the release and one that becomes the critical path.

  • The ATO as the last milestone

    Security documentation is written after the system is built, controls are retrofitted, and authorization becomes the blocker everyone saw coming.First move: agree the system boundary and control baseline in discovery

  • Accessibility retrofitted

    Section 508 and WCAG are checked before launch, when fixing one shared component means retesting every screen built on it.First move: accessibility criteria written into every story

  • Progress nobody can see

    Multi-year programs lose sponsor confidence when progress is invisible and milestones slip without warning.First move: 90-day increments with burndowns the sponsor reads

  • Interfaces nobody owns

    Case, finance and reporting systems pass data through middleware that fails quietly, and the failure surfaces as a compliance finding months later.First move: an inventory of every interface, its owner and its failure mode

Is Procyon FedRAMP-aligned?

Yes. Procyon delivers to FedRAMP Moderate and High baselines on AWS GovCloud and Azure Government, and aligns program governance to FISMA, RMF and agency-specific requirements.

Which contract vehicles does Procyon support?

GSA Schedule and major IDIQs, plus subcontracting under prime integrators. Deals are structured quickly under the right vehicle for the agency, and CMAS is available for California public-sector buyers.

Can Procyon provide cleared resources?

Yes, including Public Trust, Secret and Top Secret cleared resources across Salesforce, ServiceNow, SAP, cloud, data and AI practices.

How is citizen-facing modernization handled?

Through Salesforce Public Sector Solutions and ServiceNow Citizen Experience platforms, with omnichannel service, case management, identity verification and Section 508 and WCAG accessibility built in.

How are long public-sector programs de-risked?

Agile delivery in 90-day increments, value milestones tied to mission outcomes, transparent burndowns, ATO-aware engineering and continuous alignment with sponsors and end users.

Does working with Procyon help primes meet subcontracting goals?

Procyon is a WBENC-certified women's business enterprise and is NMSDC and SBE certified, so qualifying subcontracted work can count toward small business and supplier diversity goals.
