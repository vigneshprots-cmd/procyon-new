Data Cloud and integration, straight answers.

What does a Salesforce Data Cloud implementation involve?

Connecting your data sources, mapping them to the Data Cloud model, setting identity resolution rules to build unified profiles, applying consent and governance, then activating those profiles in Sales, Service, Marketing Cloud and Agentforce.

Do we need MuleSoft to integrate Salesforce with our ERP?

Not always. MuleSoft suits many systems and reusable APIs. Simpler flows can use native Salesforce APIs or platform events. We recommend the pattern per integration in the design phase.

Which systems can you integrate with Salesforce?

Commonly SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Workday, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Slack, WhatsApp and Okta, plus custom and legacy systems through MuleSoft or APIs.

Can Data Cloud work with Snowflake without copying data?

Yes. Zero-copy federation lets Data Cloud read from Snowflake or Databricks in place, so you avoid duplicate pipelines and keep one source of truth.

How do you handle GDPR, CCPA and HIPAA?

Consent, retention and access rules are designed into the data model and enforced before any profile is activated. Healthcare programs add HIPAA controls and Salesforce Shield encryption where required.
