What does a Salesforce consulting partner do?

A Salesforce consulting partner designs, implements, integrates and supports Salesforce for your business. Procyon covers the full cycle: implementation of Sales, Service, Revenue, Experience and Data Cloud, Agentforce AI agents, org audits and rescue, integration with ERP and other systems, and managed services after go-live.

How long does a Salesforce implementation take?

Fixed-scope starters are the fastest route: Sales Cloud goes live in 6 weeks and Service Cloud in 8 weeks. Multi-cloud programs are phased so each phase ships something usable. Our 40+ accelerators typically shorten timelines by 30 to 50% compared with building from scratch.

Can you fix a Salesforce org that another partner built?

Yes. Rescue work is a large share of our Salesforce practice. We start with an org health check across eight areas, rank every finding by business impact, then stabilize the org before rebuilding anything that blocks adoption.

Do you implement Agentforce?

Yes. Our Agent Launch Kit puts pre-configured Agentforce agents for lead qualification, case triage or self-service into production in under four weeks, grounded in your data and governed with human-in-the-loop controls.

Which Salesforce certifications does your team hold?

Procyon is a Salesforce Consulting Partner. The team holds Administrator, Platform Developer, Sales Cloud Consultant, Service Cloud Consultant, Revenue Cloud, Data Cloud and Einstein AI certifications.

How do you price Salesforce projects?

We offer fixed-scope projects, time and materials, outcome-based contracts tied to business KPIs, and managed services retainers. After a 30-minute call, a senior architect sends a written plan with scope, timeline and a cost range.

Where are your Salesforce consultants based?

Our Salesforce team works from Novato in California, Toronto in Canada and Chennai in India, so programs move across US, Canadian and Indian time zones.
