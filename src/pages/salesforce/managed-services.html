Asked before signing a managed services retainer.

What are Salesforce managed services?

An ongoing retainer in which a partner runs your Salesforce org: user support, administration, release management, monitoring and continuous improvement. Procyon offers three tiers, Run, Improve and Scale, each including the one before.

How much do Salesforce managed services cost?

Cost depends on the number of users, the clouds you run and how much change you need each month. Procyon writes each quote for your org after a short call, with no long lock-in.

How do you handle Salesforce's three releases a year?

We test the Spring, Summer and Winter release previews in your sandbox about a month before each upgrade, fix anything that would break, and plan which new features to adopt.

What hours does your support team cover?

Our hubs in Novato, Toronto and Chennai cover North American and Indian working hours, so requests logged late in a US day can be worked on overnight.

Can we switch to you from our current Salesforce partner?

Yes. We shadow your current partner, document the org, run a quick health check and take over on an agreed date, so support continues without a gap.

Is managed services better than hiring an admin?

A retainer gives you admins, developers and an architect for less than building that team in-house, with cover for holidays and turnover. Many clients keep one internal admin and use us for the rest.
