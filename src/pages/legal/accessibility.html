Accessibility Statement

The website is built to be usable by everyone, including people who use assistive technology.

1. Commitment

Procyon Technostructure aims for procyonts.com to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA.

2. How the Site Is Built

  • Semantic page structure with one main heading per page and labelled landmarks.
  • Full keyboard navigation, a skip-to-content link and visible focus states.
  • Text and interface colors chosen for readable contrast.
  • Form fields with visible labels and error messages announced to screen readers.
  • Animations reduced for visitors who turn on reduced motion in their system settings.
  • Layouts that adapt from small phones to large desktop screens and support browser zoom.

3. Known Limits

Some older articles may contain tables or images whose descriptions are still being improved. These are reviewed and updated as they are found.

4. Report a Barrier

If any part of the site is hard to use, please tell the team and include the page address. Feedback is answered within one business day.
